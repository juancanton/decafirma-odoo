The DeCA and the signed delivery note, from the outgoing transfer

For companies moving goods by road in Spain

From 5 October 2026, road freight in Spain must carry the electronic control document (DeCA) with its QR code. This module issues it in DecaFirma with one click, or automatically when the transfer is validated, using the data you already have in Odoo: customer, addresses, products, packages and weight. And the delivery note is signed by whoever receives the goods, on their phone, with nothing to install.

Everything in the DecaFirma tab of the transfer

  • Plate, trailer and driver (with their tax ID). Carrier and shipper, if they are not you.
  • The DeCA QR code in one click, the PDF, and the signing link to send to the driver or the customer.
  • Something changed? “Llevar los cambios a DecaFirma” issues a new version of the document, keeping its history.
  • Cancel with a reason, from Odoo.
  • When the customer signs, Odoo knows right away: who signed, when and any remarks, in the chatter.

Set up in Settings, in two minutes

  • Paste your DecaFirma API key and test the connection.
  • “Conectar avisos”: DecaFirma notifies Odoo instantly of every signature, new version or cancellation (HMAC-signed). If your Odoo is not reachable from the internet, a scheduled action catches up every 15 minutes.
  • Issue on validation: nothing, the DeCA, or the DeCA and the delivery note. If something is missing, the transfer is validated anyway and the reason is logged in the chatter.
  • DeCA shipper: your company or the customer of the transfer.
  • Numbering: DecaFirma's, or the Odoo transfer reference.

Delivery routes

With Inventory batch transfers, a single DeCA for the whole route and one delivery note per stop, each with its own signature. Stops can be added or removed later. In Odoo 17 to 19 this comes with decafirma_stock_batch, installed automatically together with Batch Transfers.

The paperwork, ready to invoice

  • The driver takes a photo of the consignment note or the weighbridge ticket in DecaFirma; DecaFirma reads the number and the net weight.
  • It arrives in Odoo on its own: the photo attached to the transfer and in the chatter, and the paper in Inventory > Papeles del porte, with the total weight, by month or issuer.
  • “Marcar facturado” marks it as invoiced in Odoo and in DecaFirma, so nothing is invoiced twice.
  • With the DecaFirma plans that include transport papers.

Trucks in Odoo Fleet? DecaFirma Fleet (decafirma_fleet) fills in the plate and the driver from the vehicle.

Data sent to DecaFirma

Nothing leaves Odoo until you enter your own DecaFirma API key in Settings. From then on, only when a document is issued, corrected, cancelled or sent (by a user, or on validation if you turn it on), Odoo sends DecaFirma the data the DeCA requires: company, customer, carrier and driver names, tax IDs (NIF) and addresses, vehicle plates, products, packages and weights, and the delivery slip PDF when you ask for it to be signed. From DecaFirma, Odoo receives the QR codes, PDFs, signatures and transport papers of its own shipments. Remove the API key to stop it.

Requirements

  • A DecaFirma account with API access (paid plans). The key is created in Configuración > Conectar tu programa. The module itself is free.
  • Tax IDs (NIF) of the customer, the driver and your company, and complete addresses: the DeCA requires them.
  • Modules: decafirma (the base, installed automatically) and decafirma_stock.
  • The user interface is in Spanish: the DeCA is a Spanish legal document.
