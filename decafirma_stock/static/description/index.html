The problem

Every truck that leaves needs its DeCA.

Since 5 October 2026 road freight in Spain carries the electronic control document (DeCA) with its QR code. Without it, a fine from 401 to 600 € (art. 141.17 of the LOTT).

⚠

The same data in two programs

The transfer already has the customer, the address, the products and the weight. Typing it again in another program for every load is slow.

⚠

Changes on the road

Another truck, another weight: a new paper for the driver means reaching the truck. Here the new version keeps the same QR code.

⚠

Delivery notes that do not come back

Signed on paper, they arrive days later, and the remarks of the customer get lost on the way.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

→ What do I need?

A DecaFirma account with the API: it is included in the 7-day trial and, after it, comes with the Conexión add-on. The key is created in DecaFirma, Configuración > Conectar tu programa. The modules are free. Create your DecaFirma account.

→ What data has to be in Odoo?

What the DeCA requires: the tax IDs (NIF) of the customer, the driver and your company, and complete addresses. If something is missing, DecaFirma says what and the transfer shows it.

→ Does any data leave my Odoo?

Yes, to DecaFirma, which is the one that issues the DeCA. Nothing until you enter your own API key. Then, only when a document is issued, corrected, cancelled or sent (by a user, or on validation if you turn it on): names, tax IDs and addresses, plates, products, packages and weights, and the delivery slip PDF if you sign Odoo's own. DecaFirma sends back the QR codes, PDFs, signatures and transport papers of your shipments. Remove the API key to stop it.

→ Does it work on Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online does not install apps with Python code. It works on Odoo.sh and on your own server, Community or Enterprise.

→ Can a route have several shippers?

No. One DeCA for the whole route is allowed when every stop has the same shipper and the same carrier (Resolution of 5 June 2026, section six). A transfer that already has its own DeCA is cancelled before it joins a route.

→ My trucks are in Fleet. Do I type the plates?

No. DecaFirma Fleet, free, is installed by itself with Fleet: choose the vehicle and its plate and driver go into the DeCA.

Preguntas

Preguntas frecuentes.

→ ¿Qué necesito?

Una cuenta de DecaFirma con la API: va incluida en la prueba de 7 días y, después, con el extra Conexión. La clave se crea en DecaFirma, Configuración > Conectar tu programa. Los módulos son gratis. Crea tu cuenta de DecaFirma.

→ ¿Qué datos tienen que estar en Odoo?

Los que pide el DeCA: el NIF del cliente, del conductor y de tu empresa, y las direcciones completas. Si falta algo, DecaFirma dice qué y el albarán lo enseña.

→ ¿Sale algún dato de mi Odoo?

Sí, a DecaFirma, que es quien emite el DeCA. Nada hasta que pones tu propia clave de la API. Después, solo cuando se emite, corrige, anula o manda un documento (lo hace un usuario, o al validar si lo activas): nombres, NIF y domicilios, matrículas, productos, bultos y pesos, y el PDF del albarán si firmas el de Odoo. DecaFirma devuelve los QR, los PDF, las firmas y los papeles del porte de tus envíos. Quita la clave y se acaba.

→ ¿Funciona en Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online no instala apps con código Python. Funciona en Odoo.sh y en tu propio servidor, Community o Enterprise.

→ ¿Una ruta puede tener varios cargadores?

No. Un solo DeCA para toda la ruta vale cuando todas las paradas tienen el mismo cargador y el mismo transportista (Resolución de 5 de junio de 2026, apartado sexto). Un albarán que ya tiene su DeCA suelto se anula antes de meterlo en una ruta.

→ Tengo los camiones en Flota. ¿Escribo las matrículas?

No. DecaFirma: Flota, gratuita, se instala sola con Flota: eliges el vehículo y su matrícula y su conductor van al DeCA.
