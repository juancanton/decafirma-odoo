DecaFirma Connector

The base to issue the Spanish e-CMR (DeCA) and signed delivery notes from Odoo

Stores the connection with DecaFirma (API key and webhooks), the shipments and their documents (QR code, version, status, PDF and who signed) and keeps them up to date on its own. It does nothing by itself: install DecaFirma Inventory (decafirma_stock) to issue documents from your outgoing transfers.

  • Settings: the DecaFirma API key, a connection test and the webhook set up with one button.
  • One record per DecaFirma shipment, linked to its origin (an Inventory transfer, a TMS service…), with its documents: QR code, version, status, PDF and signer.
  • Sign Odoo's own delivery slip: the usual PDF goes out, the customer signs it on their phone and it comes back signed, attached to the transfer.
  • Access rights as in DecaFirma: DecaFirma / Usuario (user) issues, corrects and sends; DecaFirma / Responsable (manager) can also cancel. DecaFirma shows who did it.
  • Transport papers: the photo of the consignment note or weighbridge ticket, with its number and net weight, attached to the transfer and ready to invoice (DecaFirma plans with papers).
  • Signed webhooks (HMAC) for every signature, new version or cancellation, plus a scheduled action every 15 minutes in case one is missed.

Data sent to DecaFirma

Nothing leaves Odoo until you enter your own DecaFirma API key in Settings. From then on, only when a user issues, corrects, cancels or sends a document, Odoo sends DecaFirma the data the DeCA requires: company, customer, carrier and driver names, tax IDs (NIF) and addresses, vehicle plates, products, packages and weights, and the delivery slip PDF when you ask for it to be signed. DecaFirma sends back the QR code, the PDFs and the signatures. Remove the API key to stop it.

Requirements

  • A DecaFirma account with API access (paid plans). The module itself is free.
  • The user interface is in Spanish: the DeCA is a Spanish legal document (Orden FOM/2861/2012, mandatory for road freight in Spain from 5 October 2026).
