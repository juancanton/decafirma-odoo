The problem

The DeCA is one more paper, and the data is already in Odoo.

Since 5 October 2026 road freight in Spain carries the electronic control document (DeCA) with its QR code (Ley 9/2025, Orden FOM/2861/2012 and Resolution of 5 June 2026).

⚠

Typing it twice

Customer, addresses, goods and weight are already in Odoo. Copying them into another program for every load is slow and brings mistakes.

⚠

A fine on the road

Driving without it is a serious infringement: from 401 to 600 € (art. 141.17 of the LOTT).

⚠

Signed papers lost

The delivery note signed on paper comes back days later, if it comes back, and nobody knows in the office whether the customer had remarks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

→ What do I need?

A DecaFirma account with the API: it is included in the 7-day trial and, after it, comes with the Conexión add-on. The modules are free. Create your DecaFirma account.

→ Does it issue anything on its own?

No. This is the base. Install DecaFirma Inventory to issue the DeCA and the delivery note from your outgoing transfers; it installs this connector with it.

→ Does any data leave my Odoo?

Yes, to DecaFirma, which is the one that issues the DeCA. Nothing until you enter your own API key. Then, only when a document is issued, corrected, cancelled or sent: the data the DeCA requires (names, tax IDs and addresses of your company, the customer, the carrier and the driver, plates, products, packages and weights) and the delivery slip PDF when you sign Odoo's own. DecaFirma sends back the QR codes, PDFs, signatures and transport papers of your shipments. Remove the API key to stop it.

→ Does it work on Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online does not install apps with Python code. It works on Odoo.sh and on your own server, Community or Enterprise.

→ What if my Odoo is not reachable from the internet?

Then the instant notices cannot arrive, and a scheduled action asks DecaFirma every 15 minutes. Ponerse al día ahora in Settings does it at once.

→ How long are the documents kept?

DecaFirma keeps them 12 months. In Odoo, the signed PDF and the transport paper photos stay attached to the transfer.

Preguntas

Preguntas frecuentes.

→ ¿Qué necesito?

Una cuenta de DecaFirma con la API: va incluida en la prueba de 7 días y, después, con el extra Conexión. Los módulos son gratis. Crea tu cuenta de DecaFirma.

→ ¿Emite algo por sí solo?

No. Es la base. Instala DecaFirma: Inventario para emitir el DeCA y el albarán desde tus albaranes de salida; ese ya instala este conector.

→ ¿Sale algún dato de mi Odoo?

Sí, a DecaFirma, que es quien emite el DeCA. Nada hasta que pones tu propia clave de la API. Después, solo cuando se emite, corrige, anula o manda un documento: los datos que pide el DeCA (nombres, NIF y domicilios de tu empresa, el cliente, el transportista y el conductor, matrículas, productos, bultos y pesos) y el PDF del albarán si firmas el de Odoo. DecaFirma devuelve los QR, los PDF, las firmas y los papeles del porte de tus envíos. Quita la clave y se acaba.

→ ¿Funciona en Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online no instala apps con código Python. Funciona en Odoo.sh y en tu propio servidor, Community o Enterprise.

→ ¿Y si mi Odoo no es accesible desde internet?

Entonces los avisos al momento no llegan, y una tarea programada pregunta a DecaFirma cada 15 minutos. Ponerse al día ahora, en Ajustes, lo hace en el acto.

→ ¿Cuánto tiempo se guardan los documentos?

DecaFirma los guarda 12 meses. En Odoo, el PDF firmado y las fotos de los papeles quedan adjuntos al albarán.
