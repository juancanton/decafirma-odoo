DecaFirma Fleet

The truck and the trailer from Fleet, straight into the Spanish DeCA

If you keep your trucks in Odoo Fleet, there is no need to type plates by hand: choose the vehicle on the outgoing transfer or on the delivery route, and its plate and its driver go into the electronic control document (DeCA) issued with DecaFirma Inventory.

  • Vehicle from Fleet: fills in the plate and the driver of that vehicle.
  • Trailer: another Fleet vehicle, whose plate goes into the DeCA.
  • The same on batch transfers (delivery routes): one truck for every stop.
  • The plate can still be edited by hand: it is what the DeCA carries.

Requirements

  • decafirma_stock (DecaFirma Inventory) and Fleet. This module is installed automatically when both are (in Odoo 17 to 19, together with DecaFirma Delivery Routes).
  • No extra data is sent to DecaFirma: only the plate and the driver, as without this module.
  • The module itself is free; DecaFirma needs an account with API access (paid plans).
